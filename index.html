AI Agents

An AI agent is like a smart team member that never sleeps. It understands your business, talks to your customers, and completes tasks on its own, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What can an AI agent do for you?

💬

Customer support

Answers common questions instantly, tracks orders, and passes tricky cases to your team with the full conversation attached.

📈

Sales assistant

Greets website visitors, recommends the right product or service, collects contact details, and books meetings. Example: a visitor asks for a price and ends up booking a call.

📅

Appointment booking

Customers book, change or cancel by chat, and automatic reminders reduce no-shows. Example: a salon fills empty slots without the owner touching the phone.

⚙️

Daily operations

Writes reports, sorts emails, updates spreadsheets, and sends invoices. Example: every evening the owner receives a summary of the day's sales and open tasks.

🌍

Many languages

Talks to customers in their own language, so you can serve more people without hiring translators.

🧑‍💼

Internal team helper

Your staff can ask it about company rules, prices, or procedures and get an instant answer. Example: a new employee learns the refund policy in seconds.

Real-life examples

Shop owner

Gets 100 messages a day asking about prices and delivery. The AI agent replies in seconds, in the customer's language, takes the order, and notifies the owner. The owner only steps in for special requests.

Clinic

The agent answers questions about opening hours and services, books appointments, and sends reminders. The receptionist focuses on the patients in front of her.

Real estate company

The agent replies to every property inquiry right away, asks about budget and location, and sends only serious buyers to the agents.

Why businesses choose it

  • Works nonstop, including nights and holidays
  • Trained on your own business data
  • Faster replies and happier customers

AI Automation

AI automation takes the boring, repeated tasks off your team's plate. The work gets done faster, the same way every time, with far fewer mistakes.

What can we automate?

📧

Email and messages

Sorts incoming mail, drafts replies, and forwards important items to the right person.

🧾

Invoices and records

Reads invoices and receipts, fills in spreadsheets, and keeps your records tidy.

📊

Reports

Collects numbers from your tools and sends a clear summary on a schedule you choose.

🔗

Connecting your tools

Links your apps so data moves between them on its own, with no copy and paste.

Why businesses choose it

  • Saves hours of manual work every week
  • Fewer human errors
  • Works around the clock without breaks
